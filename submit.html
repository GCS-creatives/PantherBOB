Tips for writing a great question

  • Start with "In which book" — that part's already filled in for you below.
  • Make it specific to one exact moment in the book — something you could point to on one page.
  • Include the page number where the answer is found.
  • Don't give away too much — avoid using character names, place names, or the book's title in your question.
  • Avoid anything too obscure or unimportant — pick a moment that matters to the story.
Example: "In which book does the main character get his brother goggles for his birthday? (p. 6)"
